Skill management

find-skillsvercel-labsSearches for existing skills that fit a task, compares them and installs the ones you approve.ask "is there a skill for…"
task-observerrebelyticsWatches your corrections and repeated work, then proposes small skill improvements. Nothing changes until you approve.needs a line in CLAUDE.md to switch on
Before installing anything: skills run with full access to your computer. Scan each one first with NVIDIA's SkillSpector: skillspector scan <GitHub link> --no-llm. A high score means "read the flagged lines", not "malware". Install from the original GitHub repo, never from a copied folder, and never share your own .claude folder.
